Use the reference as a STYLE AND PALETTE anchor. Create a genuinely different cat design, not a recolor and not a minor adjustment to the existing silhouette.

Shared art direction: bold angular origami mascot, Linux desktop / indie game character attitude, a charming but grown-up compact app icon. Broad geometric planes, crisp chamfered corners, deliberate asymmetric folds, thick confident silhouette and restrained dimensionality from two or three tonal folded facets. Clean matte flat-color image, not a real paper photograph. Keep construction simple and visually legible when scaled down.

Keep the reference's lively palette: solid lavender-lilac background approximately #BAA3EB, deep ink plum approximately #302641, warm ivory approximately #FFF6E0, saturated raspberry approximately #DF4E78. Avoid dulling the lilac or making ivory yellow. Use raspberry as a strong purposeful focal accent, with small darker plum/raspberry facets for folds. No extra colors. Background fills every edge.
One square opaque image, one large centered icon occupying about 75% of the canvas, with balanced generous clearance. No text, letters, watermark, captions, grid, border, swatches, extra props, glow, gradients, outlines or floating decoration.

Design direction: a low, wide, very compact loaf-shaped television cat, with a stocky horizontal body about 1.6 times as wide as tall, two short triangular ears integrated into its top corners and a short upturned angular tail on its right. Make this visibly different from the tall original: broad, relaxed, sturdy, playful. Two tiny chunky tucked paws fit the lower silhouette. Body and outer television frame are plum; large chamfered inset screen is ivory; one large raspberry right-facing play triangle sits centered on the screen. Raspberry folded ear interiors and a single raspberry tail-tip facet supply color rhythm. A broad subtle plum fold across one lower corner suggests folded paper. Do not add facial eyes, antennae, buttons, whiskers or fine details. The TV screen and play mark are the entire face.
